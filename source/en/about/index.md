Hi, I'm **Hank Wu** (吳承翰), a master's student in Electrical Engineering at National Tsing Hua University, advised by Prof. Chao-Tsung Huang.

What I do most is take an algorithm and move it onto hardware: first write a Python golden model and make sure the numbers are right, then find a way to make it run within limited resources. From an 8051 with only 256 bytes of RAM and a Basys 3 FPGA to the 3D Gaussian Splatting accelerator in ICLAB and an N16 back-end flow, every level comes down to the same question: can the hardware afford what the algorithm asks for?

I spend the rest of my time on the algorithms themselves, such as the MVA 2025 bird-tracking challenge and multi-frame super-resolution on a phone. Having worked on both sides, I have a clearer sense, when designing hardware, of what can be cut and what can't be touched.

This site is my work log: it collects the projects I've built and the notes I've taken along the way. If you're interested in my projects or have a collaboration opportunity, feel free to [email me](mailto:wu20040814@gmail.com), or take a look at my [CV (PDF, in Chinese)](/files/Hank-Wu-CV.pdf).
